# Seed Ideology Roster

Working roster for the knowledge base. Entries marked **°** are the ultra-niche ones —
tendencies most political tests have never heard of. Glosses are for authoring, not for
display copy.

Two taxonomy changes fall out of going this deep, both added below:
**Monetary & distributive heterodoxy** (Gesell, Douglas, Kelso — these are real, distinct
doctrines that fit nowhere else) and **Constitutional & democratic-form** (sortition,
futarchy, epistocracy — ideologies about *how decisions get made*, orthogonal to every
economic axis we have).

## Notes for authoring

**Ultra-niche entries drive the tenet vocabulary, not the other way round.** Several here
cannot be expressed with an obvious tenet set and will force new tenets during §8's step 1:

- Gesell's Freiwirtschaft needs a `money_form` tenet (`neutral`, `demurrage`,
  `commodity_backed`, `abolished`, `mutual_credit`) that nothing else in the set uses.
- Cosmism and immortalism need something like `death_and_finitude` — absurd for 395 of
  the entries and completely decisive for five.
- Sortition, futarchy and epistocracy need `decision_procedure`, which is orthogonal to
  every economic tenet and is exactly why they got their own family.
- Anthroposophy's Threefold Order needs `sphere_separation`.

This is expected and healthy: a tenet used by five ideologies and `unspecified` for the
rest is cheap (§5.2) and enormously discriminating when it fires. Resist the urge to
generalise these into something broader — that is how you blur Gesell into "monetary
reformer" and lose the entry.

**Cross-refs** mark entries that plausibly sit in two families. Pick one and let `aliases`
carry the other; the family label does not affect inference (§7).

**Expect ~30–60 of these to be merged or dropped** during authoring, once the
confusability check (§9) shows some are not separable from a neighbour by any question
worth asking. That is the process working, not a failure.
